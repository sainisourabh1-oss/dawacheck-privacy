Check any medicine. See what the same salt costs at a Jan Aushadhi Kendra.

Most branded medicines cost 50–90% less as government generics — the same active salt, verified against India's official Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) catalog. Most people never see that gap, because nobody shows it to them at the point of purchase. DawaCheck exists to close it: check a medicine, see the same-salt price ladder, and decide for yourself — with your doctor or pharmacist — whether switching makes sense.

  • Search by brand name or scan a strip/prescription photo
  • Government-verified prices where available, clearly labeled
  • Save medicines to a list and track your estimated monthly/yearly savings
  • Find your nearest Jan Aushadhi Kendra in one tap

DawaCheck is currently free to use, for a daily allowance of checks. A verified phone number is required to keep that allowance fair for everyone.
