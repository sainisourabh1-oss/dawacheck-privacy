Privacy Policy for DawaCheck

Last updated: July 25, 2026

DawaCheck ("the app") helps you compare medicine prices in India and find cheaper, government-verified alternatives. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

No accounts, no sign-up

DawaCheck does not require you to create an account, and does not collect your name, phone number, or email address.

What the app sends to our server

  • Search queries: when you search for a medicine by name, that text is sent to our server to look up prices. It is not linked to you personally — there is no user ID or device ID attached to it.
  • Photos (camera/gallery scan): when you use the scan feature, the photo is sent securely (HTTPS) to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini AI to identify the medicine. The photo itself is not stored by DawaCheck after the request completes.
  • To avoid re-processing the same request, our server keeps a small cache mapping a medicine's name to its identified details. This cache does not contain photos and is not linked to any individual user.

What stays on your phone

My List (medicines you've saved) is stored only on your own device's local storage. It is never sent to or stored on our server.

Third-party services we use

  • Google (Gemini AI) — processes photos you choose to scan and text you search for, in order to identify medicines and look up current prices (which may involve a Google Search). See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — if you tap "Find a Kendra near me," the app opens Google Maps in your browser or Maps app. DawaCheck does not share any of your data with Google to do this beyond following a link.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

DawaCheck is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised.
