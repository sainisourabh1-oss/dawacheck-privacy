Privacy Policy for DawaCheck

Last updated: July 29, 2026

DawaCheck ("the app") helps you compare medicine prices in India and find cheaper, government-verified alternatives. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

A verified phone number is required

A verified phone number is required before any part of the app can be used — this keeps the daily free allowance of checks fair for everyone and prevents automated abuse. Your phone number is verified either through Truecaller (if installed) or an SMS code, and is never guessed or trusted without server-side verification.

What we collect

  • Phone number: your verified phone number, and your daily check count (used only to enforce the free daily allowance).
  • Google account info (optional): if you choose to link a Google account — which is optional and only raises your daily check allowance, never required — we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.
  • Search queries: when you search for a medicine by name, that text is sent to our server to look up prices.
  • Photos (camera/gallery scan): when you use the scan feature, the photo is sent securely (HTTPS) to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini AI to identify the medicine. The photo itself is not stored by DawaCheck after the request completes.
  • Subscription status (optional paid tier): if you subscribe to the optional paid tier, the purchase itself is handled entirely by Google Play — we only receive confirmation that a subscription is active, never your payment details.

My List

Medicines you add to My List are saved to your phone-verified account on our server (so the list follows you if you switch phones), and cached on your device. It is not sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

From Settings → Account → "Delete my account" in the app, you can permanently remove your phone number, any linked Google info, and your saved My List from our server at any time. This also clears the local copy on your device. This action cannot be undone.

Third-party services we use

  • Truecaller — used to verify your phone number with a single tap, if you have the Truecaller app installed. See Truecaller's privacy policy.
  • Firebase (Google) — used to verify your phone number via SMS code when Truecaller isn't available. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Sign-In — used only to verify your identity if you choose to link a Google account. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google (Gemini AI) — processes photos you choose to scan and text you search for, in order to identify medicines and look up current prices (which may involve a Google Search). See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing — handles the optional paid subscription purchase. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — if you tap "Find a Kendra near me," the app opens Google Maps in your browser or Maps app. DawaCheck does not share any of your data with Google to do this beyond following a link.

Current practices

As of the date of this policy: DawaCheck does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, does not sell your personal data to third parties, and does not track you across other apps or websites. If any of this changes, we'll update this policy first (see "Changes to this policy" below) and notify users before the change takes effect.

Children's privacy

DawaCheck is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised.
